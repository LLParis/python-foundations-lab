# London's learning mission

Confirmed with London on September 19, 2026. This is the governing purpose of the
learning system. Tools, integrations, curriculum aids, and public presentation
should be evaluated by how well they advance it.

## Starting point

Start from London's actual near-zero level of independent algorithmic problem
solving. Some elementary syntax and small untimed constructions have been
demonstrated. Prior coursework, AI-built artifacts, imported platform history,
or a polished GitHub page do not substitute for personally demonstrated ability.

## Destination

1. Independently understand, derive, implement, test, debug, explain, and adapt
   solutions to unfamiliar, difficult technical interview problems, from a blank
   editor under time constraints and without AI where required.
2. Develop exceptional programming, data-structure/algorithm, software-engineering,
   systems, mathematics, and ML ability. Become a serious contributor to demanding
   engineering teams and to AI research.
3. Pursue strong SWE, AI/ML, systems, and research-engineering opportunities that
   expand those capabilities. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind are major
   targets, alongside other roles, research programs, and open-source work that
   build toward the same destination. Focus on outstanding capability and evidence.
4. Produce original, defensible advances in algorithms, optimization, learning and
   training architectures, reasoning, perception, multimodality, and the intelligence
   stack of robotics. Progress through implementation, reproduction, investigation,
   and original contributions/publications.
5. Grow into technical/research leadership, consequential ownership, and eventually
   founding an original frontier-technology company or laboratory.

## How the system serves that mission

- **GPT web remains the personalized daily teacher.** Preserve its curriculum,
  current checkpoint, feedback loop, and progressive difficulty. Codex/Astra builds
  and maintains the environment and integrations; it is not the daily lesson loop.
- Write real code in VS Code. Preserve meaningful attempts, reasoning, errors,
  repairs, and later re-solves. Keep exact handoffs easy and routine tools free of
  model calls.
- Daily use must fit repeated sessions: open the arena, write, and request review.
  Automate saving, version transfer, and receiving tutor exercises. Do not make
  London operate a checklist of commands or shuttle code between text boxes.
- Blind 75, NeetCode 150, LeetCode, Codeforces, and HackerRank provide practice and
  external evidence as prerequisites become ready. They do not replace the tutor
  or justify skipping foundations. Prepare for unfamiliar variations, not an answer bank.
- Track platform acceptance separately from assistance, cold/no-clue solving,
  retention, transfer, and timed performance. Build genuine ability and visible
  evidence without manufacturing streaks, claims, or completion percentages.
- Preserve the repository's established animated, colorful visual identity. Improve
  usefulness and presentation within that design rather than stripping it away.
- Preserve user authorship and scope. Do not complete learner exercises as setup
  work, publish private conversations, or mistake tool verification for learning.

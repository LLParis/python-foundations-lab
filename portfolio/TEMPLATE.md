# Project writeup template

Copy this only when real work exists. Remove unanswered prompts from a published
writeup or explicitly mark the corresponding evidence as unavailable.

## Problem

What problem did the project solve? What were its constraints?

## Use it

Link to the code and give commands verified against the published revision.

## My contribution and assistance

What did I personally design, implement, debug, or investigate? What was supplied
by a tutor, collaborator, AI system, library, or existing implementation?

## Design and alternatives

Explain the mechanism, a straightforward baseline, and the tradeoffs behind the
chosen approach. Include complexity or resource costs when relevant.

## Evidence

Link to relevant tests, examples, measurements, or experiments. Explain what they
establish and their limits. Keep unsuccessful approaches when they explain a
decision. Separate correctness from speed and empirical results from hypotheses.

## Limitations and next question

What does the project not establish? What remains to understand or improve?
